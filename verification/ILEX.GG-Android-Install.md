# Install ILEX.GG on Android

Version **1.17.0-android.25** includes the complete Scarlet & Violet era, with interactions scoped to Black & White onward. Unlimited remains deferred.

Update: install this APK over your existing ILEX.GG app to keep its workspace and cached images. No uninstall is needed.

1. Transfer `ILEX.GG-Android.apk` to your Android phone or tablet and open it.
2. If Android asks, allow installation from the app opening the file. Launch **ILEX.GG** from your app drawer afterward.
3. Requires Android 8.0 or newer and a current Android System WebView/Chrome.

**Controls:** Tap a field Pokémon for attacks, Powers and Retreat. Hold a revealed card to enlarge it. Tap a hand card and choose **Place on table**, then a highlighted destination, or drag it onto the table. Swipe horizontally through your hand. Energy payments and effect choices retain their prompts. Expand **Match controls** for End turn, Undo and bot controls. Android Back dismisses previews and returns to the menu.

**Landscape:** Both sides, Benches, piles and your hand fit on one screen. Match controls and effect choices open over the table. Large hands swipe horizontally. Portrait remains scrollable.

**Bring your decks:** Use **Export workspace** in Windows, transfer the JSON, then use **Import workspace** in Android. Import replaces the Android workspace. There is no automatic synchronization.

**Offline images:** Base–Fossil and the rotating Celebi images are bundled. Other card images download as viewed. Use **Download card images** on the main menu to cache the full collection (about 2.5 GB); keep the app open during bulk downloads. Downloaded images, rules, cards and decks remain available offline.

The APK's signatures, integrity, 112 Chromium mobile UI checks, SV checks at three landscape sizes and 38 package/static checks passed. Physical Android/emulator acceptance remains pending; native file pickers, cache downloads and real-device lifecycle/audio still need on-device confirmation.

This APK has local/bot play; Windows multiplayer beta is a separate download.
