# Sword & Shield rules and printed-data sources

Scope: all 3,715 English Sword & Shield printings in the 28 bundled catalogs, including 307 Black Star Promos, special expansions and subsets, Futsal 2020 and McDonald's 2021–22. Interactions cover Black & White onward through implemented Sword & Shield. Unlimited and later-era automation are deferred. Earlier historical profiles are retained. Format legality and bans are separate from mechanics; no current Expanded banlist is supplied.

Printed records come from the cached pokemon-tcg-data English catalogs; the generated inventory preserves source identifiers and printing identity. SWSH299–301 supplement records are corroborated by the corresponding [Jirachi](https://www.serebii.net/card/swshpromos/299.shtml), [Unown](https://www.serebii.net/card/swshpromos/300.shtml) and [Lugia](https://www.serebii.net/card/swshpromos/301.shtml) records. Existing bundled scans are reused; no new scans are needed for this era.

The official [Sword & Shield rulebook](https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2-en-uk/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/swsh10_rulebook_en.pdf), particularly Appendices A–C, governs Radiant, VSTAR and V-UNION rules. Individual V-UNION pieces expose only their permitted name/type/category characteristics; assembly requires matching artwork, four distinct quarters and an empty Bench space, with each name assembled once per game. An assembled card is neither Basic nor Evolution, has its Rule Box and grants three Prizes. VSTAR use is shared between attacks and Abilities. Radiant deck limits apply across names.

The official [V-UNION introduction](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/pokemon-v-union-arrive-in-the-pokemon-tcg) and [product description](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/product-gallery/pokemon-v-union-special-collection-mewtwo-greninja-zacian/) corroborate the assembly mechanic. The [official errata list](https://play.pokemon.com/en-gb/resources/documents/tcg-errata/) corrects Cinderace 36's Retreat Cost to one and specifies opponent attacks for Garchomp's Sonic Slip. Greninja V-UNION's printed attack is Waterfall Bind; the local catalog spelling was corrected against its scan.

Native implementations and shared interpreters both register against exact printed attacks, HP, costs and characteristics. Repairs include Energy-unit accounting on physical Special Energy cards, V-family queries, blockable attack knockouts, defensive effect attribution, Ability probe recursion, conditional effects and bot prompt choices. Four V-UNION scans are composed in a two-by-two grid for tabletop and enlarged views; no card artwork is regenerated.

The shared copy interpreter consumes VSTAR use for compiled copied attacks, while native delegated attacks retain their own use handling. Attack metadata carries the Lost Zone prerequisite into borrowed attacks and preflight validation. Nine cross-era checks cover BW Mew-EX borrowing native and compiled VSTAR attacks, spent-use rejection before animation, copied Star Requiem's prerequisite, and Tool-granted VSTAR independence from GX use and Path to the Peak. Separate current bridge/bot checks cover opponent-attaching XY Tools. Exact tactical assertions use a fixed strategy clock to avoid CPU-load cutoffs; complete bot games and native worker checks retain production timing.

Evidence is finite: expected-result scenarios, all printed attacks, bounded branch variants, Ability/Trainer availability audits, identity and native-guard checks, scoped cached-deck games, replay/Undo, shared historical regressions and native UI checks. Unavailable passive or conditional fixtures are recorded separately and do not prove their positive branches. Android WebView-style preview and SDK verification do not constitute physical-device acceptance. Pilot reports and canceled intermediate runs are historical diagnostics, not final release evidence.
