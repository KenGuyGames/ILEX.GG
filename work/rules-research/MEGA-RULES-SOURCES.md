Existing cached catalogs and printed-text records are indexed by `mega-inventory.json`; they are source material, not validation. New image source bytes, decoded PNG hashes, dimensions, observed URLs and acquisition manifests are retained in `work/card-sources/mega-official-promos/source-images.json`. JPEG XL originals were decoded without artwork reconstruction or resizing. Some official bird promo images are only 384×535; that source limitation is preserved.

Promo effect counterparts are verified separately from printing identity. MEP 092 uses the 2026 Psyduck retreat text rather than earlier Paradise Resort text. Later announced promos are cached only, and unofficial/non-numbered RGB cache rows are excluded. No official banlist or tournament legality has been invented.


Scope clarification: MEP102–110 are included as officially announced 30th product previews, with future release dates retained.
