# ILEX.GG 2.0 — The Grove Reimagined

Cinematic emerald forest, warm pale gold and luminous mint. Original environmental artwork supplies depth; opaque teal surfaces protect contrast. The rotating Celebi printing remains the visual anchor. Every existing mode stays in the hub. Real card/deck counts replace decorative statistics.

A shared typography, surface, focus, input, card, dialog and button system covers the gallery, deck builder, formats, custom cards, cubes, packs, private multiplayer and Pocket. The desktop paper arena sizes to remaining viewport height with a wide physical playmat; Android retains its tested landscape layout. Elemental presentation adds charge sigils, orbiting energy, a public attacker portrait, radial lighting and shockwaves. Actual engine outcomes, coin faces, queues, Skip and recovery guards remain unchanged.

Ambient particles run only on home, at a bounded 25 frames/second and capped device resolution. Hidden/paused pages, reduced motion and disabled cinematic effects stop the loop. Keyboard focus, touch targets, scans, printing identities and all data/state schemas are retained. No gameplay engine changes, online transport changes, profile migrations or publication in this update.

Physical Android acceptance remains pending. Design is AAA-inspired presentation, not a claim of production-studio resources or exhaustive device certification.
