# Promo foil presentation

The bundled `promo-finishes.json` contains finish metadata for 1,790 indexed entries across promotional collections, POP, McDonald's and related sets. Records were obtained from the English TCGdex card API on October 13, 2026. Endpoint: `https://api.tcgdex.net/v2/en/cards/{cardId}`. The importer reads `variants` and standard-sized `variants_detailed` finish types and excludes pricing data. `work/desktop-build/promo-finishes.py` regenerates this data and retains reviewed corrections. The audit report is `work/rules-research/promo-finish-report.json`.

The resulting records identify 1,000 entries with holo or reverse-holo availability, 790 without, and 107 with both normal and foil finishes. This is source-metadata coverage, not independent certification of every physical finish or language/stamp/size variant. The app has one indexed scan per entry. Mixed-finish entries offer a saved cosmetic Animated foil choice in the inspector; it toggles the overlay while keeping the scan, deck card ID and quantities unchanged.

Two upstream missing holo flags were corrected using the bundled English scans: Venusaur promo 13 (`655cad4e0523dac4f57ad71b.webp`) and Cool Porygon promo 15 (`233318e1e8917e008ca5ad88.webp`). Their visible foil backgrounds were reviewed directly. These corrections are explicit in the generation script and audit report. Corroborating card records: [Venusaur](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Venusaur_%28Wizards_Promo_13%29) and [Cool Porygon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cool_Porygon_%28Wizards_Promo_15%29). Actual bundled scans were also reviewed for Team Rocket's Meowth 18 and Scizor 33, whose source foil classifications were retained; assumptions based only on card names were not used.

Examples checked against the English source records include normal Mew promo 8 versus holo Mew promo 9, Eevee promo 11's mixed finishes, reverse-holo Entei promo 34, modern SWSH001, and the five reverse-foil versus four plain Best of Game entries. [Best of Game release information](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Best_of_Game_%28TCG%29) corroborates that distinction.

These are decorative overlays with stylized artwork/full-front masks. They do not add card-rule implementations or claim exact reproduction of every physical foil texture. Native tests cover pointer movement, hidden-card exclusion, no game-state mutation, reduced-motion preferences and saved finish choices.
