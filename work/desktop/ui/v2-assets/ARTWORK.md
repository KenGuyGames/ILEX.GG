## Final prompt

Create an original production game environment background image for ILEX.GG, a premium fantasy trading card game interface. Ultra-wide 16:9 composition, cinematic richly painted 3D game key art, ancient lush forest at blue hour, enormous moss-covered trees framing both sides, arching roots, a luminous emerald clearing and circular old stone arena softly visible on the right, shafts of warm pale gold sunlight from high right, subtle floating golden spores, layered turquoise mist and detailed fern silhouettes in foreground, deep rich midnight teal shadows. Strong tasteful contrast, beautiful atmospheric depth, sophisticated AAA game art, inviting and magical rather than sinister. Left third is darker and has quiet open negative space for large readable interface typography; focal illumination right of center behind a separate card showcase that will be composited later. Environment artwork ONLY, no characters, no creatures, no cards, no lettering, no words, no logos, no UI or frames. Make it beautiful at full desktop width and suitable for cropping on phones.
