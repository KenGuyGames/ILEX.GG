# ILEX.GG feature backlog

Requested by Kenneth on October 12, 2026. These are planned features, to implement when Kenneth is ready. Add subsequent feature ideas to this same list; retain existing items and record their status. This list does not authorize beginning implementation now.

- [ ] **Odds checker:** Calculate the probability of starting a selected card in the opening hand and the probability of prizing it. Account for the selected deck and number of copies.
- [x] **Interactive foil overlay:** Implemented October 13, 2026. Known holo-or-higher printings get pointer-reactive sheen and sparkle in the library, visual decks, face-up tabletop cards, inspectors and magnified previews. Vintage holo masks cover the artwork area. Non-holo and hidden cards are excluded; reduced-motion preferences disable movement. Unknown rarity is not guessed. Promo support uses 1,790 finish records, including 1,000 entries with holo/reverse-holo availability and reviewed Venusaur/Cool Porygon corrections. Mixed-finish promo entries have a saved animated-foil on/off choice in the inspector.
- [ ] **Opponent pile counts:** When hovering over the opponent's deck or discard pile, clearly display the number of cards in that pile.
- [ ] **Online multiplayer:** Allow online play without requiring payment for hosting or other infrastructure. Evaluate feasible approaches and their connection requirements when implementation begins. Online Undo Action must send a request to the opponent, who can approve or deny it; do not rewind either player before approval. Requested October 13, 2026.
- [x] **Undo Action:** Implemented October 13, 2026 for local rules matches and bot games. Restores the last human decision, automatic effects, and subsequent bot responses using the saved journal. Works with pending choices and finished matches; bot undo pauses the bot without requesting approval. Future online multiplayer undo requires opponent approval or denial; that networking work remains pending until multiplayer is implemented.
- [x] **Audio:** Implemented October 13, 2026. Two original calm forest themes for menus and three retro battle tracks, with automatic scene switching and a manual track selector. Committed human/bot moves and manual tabletop actions have synthesized sound effects. Separate music/effect volumes, enable switches and mute persist after restart. Playback starts on interaction; undo replay and rejected moves stay silent.
- [x] **Attack and ability search:** Implemented October 13, 2026. The library matches card names/IDs, attack names, and ability/Pokémon Power names using bundled or cached text. Search scopes, match hints, set/custom filters, partial names, case/accent normalization and pagination are supported. Custom card names are indexed too. Entries without available text retain name/ID search. Requested October 12, 2026.
- [ ] **Actions on an enlarged Pokémon:** Clicking a Pokémon to use an attack or ability opens an enlarged card view. Select the action directly on the card's attack or ability area, such as clicking Damage Swap on Alakazam to activate it. Requested October 12, 2026.

- [x] **Visual decklists:** Implemented October 13, 2026. My decks shows scans grouped into Pokémon, Trainer and Energy cards, with copy badges, quantity controls, inspection and right-click magnification. Custom cards without artwork and unavailable scans have readable placeholders. Limitless import/export remains available. Requested October 12, 2026.

- [x] **Remove formats:** Implemented October 13, 2026. Remove built-in or custom formats, choose a replacement for affected decks or leave them unassigned, and create new formats afterward. Removing all formats is supported. Deck cards and existing match rules are preserved; removed presets stay removed after restart.

Latest selected backlog work: audio, completed October 13, 2026. The other four items remain pending. WOTC implementation coverage is complete at 1,793/1,793 entries. Unlimited and later-era interaction validation remain deferred.

- [x] **Filter decks by format:** Implemented October 13, 2026. My decks has a format dropdown showing only decks passing the selected format’s size, copy limits, allowed sets, bans, Basic Pokémon and custom-card rules, regardless of their assigned format. All decks retains access to drafts; filtering never deletes decks.

- [x] **Format-first match setup:** Match format is selected before either deck. Bot and local matches list only decks assigned to that exact format, even when names or legal cards overlap. Switching formats clears stale selections; unassigned/deleted-format decks remain saved but are excluded. Match specs retain the format ID and name.
